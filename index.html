        Good to know

        Questions, answered.

        Do you offer delivery?

        Delivery depends on your area and order. Send us a message or visit the store and our team will confirm areas, charges, minimum orders and times.

        How do I find out about this week's specials?

        Our specials are listed on this page and change regularly. You can also ask our team in store or message us on WhatsApp.

        Can I place an order before I come in?

        Yes. Get in touch with the department you need, tell us what you're after, and we'll confirm availability so you don't make a wasted trip.

        Who can buy from the bottle store?

        Alcohol is sold to persons aged 18 and over only. We may ask for identification.
